## Overview

The Free Spins feature in WynnBET's casino gaming experience was suffering from usability issues, as users struggled to locate their free spins or understand how to use them. I redesigned the Free Spins experience, enhancing visibility, navigation, and user flow to create a more seamless experience for desktop and mobile platforms.

## Problem

Users were frustrated by the lack of visibility and awareness regarding their free spins, often missing out on spins that expired without notice. Many users expressed that it was unfair, and they had lost their spins unknowingly.

This led to confusion, reduced engagement, and frequent requests for compensation from customer support. Our legacy platform struggled with user retention due to poor transparency around spin availability and expiration.

## Research & Insights - Where did my free spins go?

#### User research findings

Through customer service interactions and user feedback, we discovered that users were not only unaware of their available free spins, but they also felt it was unfair when spins expired without their knowledge. Many users requested compensation for lost spins, which pointed to a significant gap in visibility and communication.

## Challenges

#### User

Spins could only be tracked inside the exact game that awarded them. There was no centralized place to see what you had, so spins went unused and engagement dropped.

We restructured the experience around centralized visibility: dedicated Free Spins surfaces reachable from any part of the platform, so a spin is never more than a tap away.

#### Technical

Screen real estate was the first constraint: the Free Spins banner needed a home prominent enough to be seen without cluttering the casino's most valuable screen. The second was operational: which games offer free spins is decided by casino management, so the feature had to integrate with the casino's CMS and let ops swap games in and out, with changes reflected immediately on both the website and the app.

<CmsFlow />

We built a universal notification system that surfaces available spins anywhere on the platform, and worked with the backend team so CMS updates flow live to every surface without a release.

#### Business

Customer service was compensating users with bonus spins for opportunities they never saw, a recurring cost driven purely by missed visibility. Making spins impossible to miss was as much a cost fix as a UX fix: fewer tickets, fewer comped bonuses, a more sustainable rewards system.

## Design Solution

I streamlined the user experience by introducing a dedicated Free Spins page, enhancing visibility and ease of navigation across both desktop and mobile platforms. The answer, in one move: take the spin out of its game and surface it in three places.

<SpinSurfaces />

#### Home page visibility

Given the limited space available for the Free Spins banner, we strategically placed it on the homepage of the casino section where users spent the most time. This ensured that the banner would be seen without distracting from the main content. The placement decision was based on analytics and user behavior patterns, prioritizing areas of the interface that garnered the most user engagement.

This ensured that users were immediately notified of their spins upon entering the casino section, addressing the previous lack of visibility.

<SpinsPrototype />

#### User profile display

A dedicated tab in the user profile where users could track all available spins and the games associated with them, ensuring continuous awareness.

When a user has zero spins, the tab will be disabled with no number displayed. Opening My Bonuses surfaces the new Bonus Spins entry directly, and the profile header counts every available spin, shown below on desktop and mobile.

<FreeSpinsMoment story="account" caption="Finding Bonus Spins: the account menu and the spin counter" />

#### Dedicated page

A dedicated page displayed all the casino games for which the user had free spins. If the user had more than one spin across different games, each game tile would show the number of available spins.

This clear layout simplified the process for users to identify which games to play to use their spins, improving overall usability and transparency. Each game card tracks its spins on a progress ring with the expiry always visible; finished games move into Past Spins, where completed and expired outcomes stay distinct.

#### Branding for marketing

In addition to improving user awareness, we also developed a unique brand identity for the Free Spins feature, including a custom icon and bold design elements. This branding not only made the feature more recognizable within the platform but also allowed us to collaborate with the marketing team to help promote it externally.

By leveraging this visual identity, the marketing team could more easily create campaigns to attract new users and highlight the Free Spins feature as a central engagement tool.

The redesign led to significant improvements in user awareness and engagement with the Free Spins feature.

While we did not track specific metrics like user retention, the significant drop in support requests indicated that users were engaging with the Free Spins feature more effectively, without needing assistance from customer service.

The improved CMS integration also allowed casino management to update the games offering free spins without additional backend issues, streamlining operations.

#### Navigation efficiency

Users found it easier to locate the free spins banner when it appeared on the main casino screen upon eligibility. This banner featured a custom-designed Free Spins icon, creating a unique brand identity for the feature.

The banner also dynamically displayed the number of free spins available, allowing users to instantly see how many spins they had, enhancing both visibility and engagement.

#### Dedicated page

A dedicated free spins page displaying all games eligible for a free spin and the remaining amount. This page only appears if they have a free spin available. Once all spins are used, the page will be removed.

#### Operational efficiency

Users were no longer limited to discovering spins only within the awarding game, leading to increased usage and spin redemptions. After the update, there was a measurable decrease in customer support tickets related to spin availability, and overall engagement with free spins improved.

Although exact numbers are unavailable due to the company's shutdown, the reduction in missed spins directly contributed to operational efficiency and cost savings.

## Next Steps / Learnings

Going forward, we could explore additional features like timed notifications or email reminders for free spins to further ensure users are aware of time-sensitive rewards.

#### A/B testing

A/B testing could focus on which notification strategies drive the highest engagement and explore how personalized notifications (based on user preferences) or different timing options could further increase engagement and reduce the chances of missed spins.
