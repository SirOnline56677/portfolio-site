## Overview

The Free Spins feature in WynnBET's casino gaming experience was suffering from usability issues, as users struggled to locate their free spins or understand how to use them. I redesigned the Free Spins experience, enhancing visibility, navigation, and user flow to create a more seamless experience for desktop and mobile platforms.

## Problem

Users were frustrated by the lack of visibility and awareness regarding their free spins, often missing out on spins that expired without notice. Many users expressed that it was unfair, and they had lost their spins unknowingly.

This led to confusion, reduced engagement, and frequent requests for compensation from customer support. Our legacy platform struggled with user retention due to poor transparency around spin availability and expiration.

## Solutions

I streamlined the user experience by introducing a dedicated Free Spins page, enhancing visibility and ease of navigation across both desktop and mobile platforms. By leveraging intuitive layouts and clear visual cues, I ensured users could locate their free spins efficiently, reducing user confusion and increasing engagement.

## Research & Insights

#### User research findings

Through customer service interactions and user feedback, we discovered that users were not only unaware of their available free spins, but they also felt it was unfair when spins expired without their knowledge. Many users requested compensation for lost spins, which pointed to a significant gap in visibility and communication.

## User Challenge

#### Challenge

Users were frustrated because they could not track their free spins unless they were in the exact game that awarded them. This lack of a centralized system resulted in missed opportunities and lowered engagement.

#### Approach

We restructured the Free Spins experience to centralize visibility and ensure users could always track their spins. This included designing dedicated sections for free spins, which could be accessed from any part of the platform, providing clarity and boosting engagement.

## Technical Challenge

#### Challenge

The system only notified users about their free spins if they were actively playing the specific game that awarded the spin. Outside of this game, there was no way for users to see or track their free spins, leading to confusion and missed opportunities to engage with the feature. Additionally, navigating between multiple sections of the platform to track free spins wasn't user-friendly.

A major constraint was the limited space available for displaying the Free Spins banner. We needed to carefully choose the optimal location where users spent the most time on the screen without cluttering the interface. It was important to ensure the banner was prominent enough to grab attention, while not interfering with the rest of the user experience.

In addition to the visibility issue and location, the backend needed to seamlessly integrate with the casino's CMS to determine which games could offer free spins. Casino management wanted the ability to easily switch games in and out of the free spins feature, which added another layer of complexity. Ensuring that this system worked flawlessly across both the website and app platforms was crucial.

#### Approach

We solved this issue by creating a universal notification system that would alert users to available spins, even when they weren't in the game that awarded them. Additionally, we placed prominent navigation cues in the user profile and the main menu to make spins accessible from any part of the platform.

We collaborated closely with the backend team to ensure the CMS integration allowed for dynamic updates, enabling management to control which games offered free spins. This flexibility was crucial for the casino's operational needs. Our team also ensured that the data fed accurately into both the app and website, so users always saw the correct games offering free spins in real time.

## Business Challenge

#### Challenge

The customer service team frequently offered users free bonuses or additional spins to compensate for missed opportunities. This was primarily due to users being unaware of expiring spins. By improving visibility, we aimed to reduce the amount of customer service intervention and unnecessary bonus spin giveaways, leading to a more sustainable and efficient rewards system.

#### Approach

We prioritized giving users better visibility of their free spins and reducing the need for customer service to compensate them with additional rewards. This solution helped cut costs related to issuing free spins or bonuses while ensuring users didn't miss their available spins.

## Design Solution

#### Home page visibility

Given the limited space available for the Free Spins banner, we strategically placed it on the homepage of the casino section where users spent the most time. This ensured that the banner would be seen without distracting from the main content. The placement decision was based on analytics and user behavior patterns, prioritizing areas of the interface that garnered the most user engagement.

This ensured that users were immediately notified of their spins upon entering the casino section, addressing the previous lack of visibility.

#### Branding for marketing

In addition to improving user awareness, we also developed a unique brand identity for the Free Spins feature, including a custom icon and bold design elements. This branding not only made the feature more recognizable within the platform but also allowed us to collaborate with the marketing team to help promote it externally.

By leveraging this visual identity, the marketing team could more easily create campaigns to attract new users and highlight the Free Spins feature as a central engagement tool.

#### Dedicated page

A dedicated page displayed all the casino games for which the user had free spins. If the user had more than one spin across different games, each game tile would show the number of available spins.

This clear layout simplified the process for users to identify which games to play to use their spins, improving overall usability and transparency.

#### Backend integration

We worked to integrate the casino's CMS with our platform, ensuring that games offering free spins could be updated dynamically by casino management. This integration allowed for greater flexibility in which games participated in the Free Spins feature, ensuring that changes made on the backend were reflected immediately across both the website and app.
