## Overview

Wrist Check is a peer-to-peer marketplace empowering watch enthusiasts to securely buy, sell, and trade luxury timepieces.

With features designed to enhance transparency, build community reputation, and provide educational support, Wrist Check addresses the specific challenges newcomers face in the secondary watch market, reducing risks associated with counterfeit and gray market watches.

## Problem

With an estimated 40 million counterfeit Swiss watches sold annually, newcomers to the high-end watch market often feel intimidated and uncertain, facing the daunting risk of purchasing fake or 'Frankenstein' watches.

Without industry knowledge, they often lack the resources to verify a watch's authenticity, leading to distrust and apprehension within this growing community.

### Solution

Wrist Check provides a comprehensive platform that empowers users to verify the authenticity of watches through educational content, trusted community reviews, and expert resources.

By offering transparent listings, reputation tracking for sellers, and educational tools, Wrist Check bridges the knowledge gap for newcomers while creating a trusted marketplace for seasoned enthusiasts.

## Technical Challenge

### Challenge

One of the main challenges was developing a system for watch authentication that users could rely on without having the watch in hand. Authenticating high-end watches in the secondary market typically requires expert inspection, which poses a problem for online transactions.

Users had to rely heavily on photos and online research, often using forums and Google to compare similar images and ask questions. This lack of physical verification created a trust gap that was difficult to bridge.

### Approach

To address this, we implemented a two fold solution: detailed photo guidelines and community-driven verification. First, sellers are required to upload high-resolution images of specific watch features that are commonly used to verify authenticity, such as serial numbers, engravings, and movement details.

We also developed an educational guide to help users know what to look for in these photos. Second, we encouraged community feedback and discussion around each listing, allowing users to ask questions directly within the platform. This created an additional layer of trust and empowered users to help each other assess authenticity, even in an online setting.

## Design Solution

Together, these features foster a transparent, trustworthy environment that encourages users to engage confidently with the secondary watch market.

### Verified seller badges

Verified sellers are not only recognized but also receive discounts on seller and buyer fees, incentivizing them to maintain high standards and transparency in their transactions.

### Mandatory rating system

Wrist Check employs a mandatory rating system to ensure accountability for every transaction. After each purchase, both the buyer and seller must provide feedback on the transaction. This system is designed to enhance credibility by allowing future users to review ratings and make informed decisions based on past interactions.

By making ratings compulsory, Wrist Check reinforces transparency and discourages dishonest behavior, fostering a safer marketplace.

### Educational course for watch buyers

To support users—particularly newcomers—Wrist Check offers an educational course on high-end watches. This course covers the basics of watch identification, tips for spotting counterfeit components, and an overview of secondary market pricing. By equipping users with knowledge, Wrist Check empowers them to make informed purchases and feel more confident in navigating the secondary market.

The course also promotes a more educated community, reducing the risks associated with counterfeit or 'Frankenstein' watches.

## Results

### Anticipated outcomes

Although no user metrics were collected, the features developed for Wrist Check are expected to build trust and enhance user confidence in the secondary watch market. The verified seller badges and mandatory rating system would likely reduce fraudulent transactions and encourage honest behavior among users.

Additionally, the educational course is anticipated to empower new buyers with knowledge, enabling them to make informed purchasing decisions and spot potential counterfeits more effectively. Together, these solutions create a foundation for a transparent, community-driven marketplace where users feel secure in their transactions.

## Next Steps / Learnings

Future steps could include conducting user testing and collecting feedback to refine Wrist Check's features based on real user experiences, further validating its impact in building trust.

A key takeaway from this project was the importance of fostering trust and transparency in high-stakes online marketplaces. Building features that promote community verification and user education are essential for establishing a credible platform in niche luxury markets.
