## Overview

Bingo AI is designed to connect older adults with flexible, accessible work opportunities, empowering them to maintain independence, improve mental and physical well-being, and secure supplemental income.

By addressing the unique challenges older adults face, Bingo AI aims to create a welcoming job platform that aligns with the needs of a growing aging population.

**Project criteria** — Use of Emerging Technology, Forward-Thinking, Innovation Alignment with Challenge(s), Impact, Presentation & Pitch.

## Challenge

How can we help older adults stay in their homes longer while maintaining their independence in a safer, healthier, and more cost-effective way?

## Solution

Our solution included implementing a user-friendly, voice-supported onboarding process, simple application steps, and accessible job matching tailored to older adults' needs.

## Persona

Emma, a 67-year-old with basic to intermediate technology skills, seeks flexible work opportunities. While she has some familiarity with using smartphones and computers, she finds complex applications intimidating. Her motivation is a mix of financial need and the desire to stay active, making her an ideal user for a platform that offers a simple, accessible interface. Emma's goals include supplementing her income on a part-time basis, connecting with others through meaningful work, and maintaining a sense of independence.

### Mental health

Bored with her everyday routine, she wants activities where she can socialize. Living off retirement funds makes her worried about her future financial situation.

### Goals

- Having a paycheck coming in from time to time.
- Opportunities to try new things.
- Stay active and engaged with others.
- Sharper mental capacity.

## Technical Challenge

### Challenge

Developing a voice and visual onboarding process for older adults presented unique technical challenges, especially as we needed to ensure compatibility with various devices and accessibility standards.

Integrating voice recognition required accurate speech processing that could handle diverse accents and speech patterns among older users.

### Approach

To address this, we planned to incorporate a reliable voice API and designed a user interface with large, easy-to-read text and intuitive navigation.

We would need to test across multiple devices to ensure a seamless experience for all users, prioritizing accessibility and ease of use.

## Business Challenge

### Challenge

Bingo AI aimed to connect older adults with part-time and contract roles, tapping into a growing demographic seeking flexible work opportunities. Our business challenge was to make the platform attractive for companies offering these roles while planning for growth in line with an aging population.

As more people live longer and healthier lives, the demand for such opportunities is expected to rise, creating a sustained market for part-time labor.

### Approach

Our approach was to create a scalable, appealing platform for partner companies while addressing the growing need for flexible work options among an aging population.

The goal was to create sustainable revenue streams while aligning with the mission of empowering older adults.

## Design Solution

### Stress free onboarding

We designed a user-friendly visual UI alongside a voice UI, with voice transcription for users who are hard of hearing.

We chose colors with enough contrast and large text to support users with impaired vision. Seniors with impaired hearing or arthritis can have what's on the screen read aloud to them and respond through voice transcription at the click of a button.

### No fill out for opening job applications

With our AI algorithm, the list of skills that is displayed on our users profiles updates each time after completing their job and reviewed by their employer. Propping the users profile for better matching jobs for future jobs and never needing the user to update their profile. The user will be able to view their skills and profile rating.

## Impact

### Hackathon winner

Bingo AI won Go Studio × InComm Payments' first-ever Innovation Jam Hackathon, judged on use of emerging technology, forward thinking, innovation alignment with the challenge, impact, and the presentation & pitch.

### Physical & mental health

Working, proactive seniors are healthier. A study shows that people who worked past age 65 were about three times more likely to report being healthy and less likely to have serious physical health problems, such as cancer or heart disease.

Older adults with frequent social contact have 70% less cognitive decline than those with lower social activities. Having seniors work is great for their mental health.

### Revenue growth

Helps to fill open positions, and could help achieve revenue growth by hiring these older adults. A study estimates that a 5% engagement increase would lead to about a 3% revenue growth for companies.

### Productive drive

Working seniors are better employees. A study shows employees aged 55+ are more engaged to their jobs than the younger employees.

### No waiting time

It has a potential to help more than 50 million seniors in the US.

## Next Steps / Learnings

We plan to refine the voice interaction further, adding personalized job suggestions based on user feedback and past applications.

Additionally, we aim to integrate wellness check-ins, helping users stay motivated and connected with the platform.

### Work from anywhere

Some seniors may not be able to leave their home. We may want to add Mechanical Turk-style options so they can work from home.

### Expanding to large corporations

We may consider expanding from small businesses to large corporations, giving seniors more options to find work.
